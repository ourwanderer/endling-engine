ELON'S FINGERTIP
/ EE-lon's FING-er-tip /
proper noun - geographical feature
Big round lake in the centre of The Continent. The story goes that one of the Dead Gods (Elon) made it by pushing his finger into the ground. It was probably a comet though.
For those who hail from the West, near the great lake of Elon's Fingertip and the jungles of The Great Verdant , Dragons are the beast of choice.
