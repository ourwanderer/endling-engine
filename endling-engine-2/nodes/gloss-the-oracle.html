THE ORACLE
/ ORACK-ul /
proper noun , prophesied being
One of two beings prophesied to bring about either the end or beginning of all creation. The other is The Exile.
"Among the maelstrom of her dreams, a few emerge that speak of power. They whisper it in sweet seduction, or hurl themselves in furious torrent, or brand her with their white-hot craving. Oracle. Exile. Alpha. Endling." , From 'Visions of The Endling' by Jiro the Monk.
ALL STRANDS , PROPHESIED / SIGNIFICANCE: ABSOLUTE
