NEW KYUSHU — THE CROW

DORAN

Koa's crow. Foul-mouthed, loyal, and considerably smarter than he lets on.

He has dreams too. In his dreams there is a man. The man is from a place that doesn't exist yet, or no longer exists, or both.

In the other reality — the one with three moons and ancient forests — there is a man also called Doran. He is not a crow there. He dreams of a girl called Koa.

Myths survive Engine traversal better than facts.
