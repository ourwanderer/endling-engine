DORAN

A foul-mouthed talking crow. Best friend to Koa (aka The Endling).

"I was there you know. I was there the day Niita set her wolves loose on my friend." , Doran

In another reality , High Wail Rook, on the rain-soaked shores of the Mourning Sea , there is a young man also called Doran SonLogan. He is from Enon Derris, a town in the wet, grey part of High Wail called The Souse. He dreams of a girl called Koa. He does not know what she is.

"High Wail is wet and grey and cold, and The Souse is the wet, grey, cold part of it. We who live here like to think we counter the weather with friendliness, but like all people the friendliness is only a mask, thick as cobweb." , Doran

Myths survive Engine traversal better than facts. Doran has survived everything.
