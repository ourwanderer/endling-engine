THE CONTINENT — THE LEGEND

HIGH WAIL ROOK

In Koa's world it is mythology. In another reality it is history. In another, it is fiction written by someone who almost remembers.

"I'm far from a lord. High Wail is wet and grey and cold and The Souse is the wet, grey, cold part of it."

Here, the Dead Gods are not metaphor. The Holy Lines are not superstition. The Engines were built by something, and that something left without explanation.
