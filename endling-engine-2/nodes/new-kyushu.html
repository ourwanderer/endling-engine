THE CITY-NATION

NEW KYUSHU

Huge city-nation. The centre of Koa's world. Feudalist in structure, consumerist in culture, warrior-class in mythology.

The air is toxic if breathed for longer than a few seconds, so everyone breathes 'gas' through helmet-mounted ventilators when outside. The helmets can also be ritualistic and have religious connotations.

The Ookami are televised. Their hunts are sponsored. The celebrities of New Kyushu are the people who kill other people, professionally and on camera.

Koa was the most celebrated of them. Then she stole a f**k load of money from a powerful War Baron and went on the run.
