HIGH WAIL ROOK — THE SWIFT

KELAK

General of the Eastern Reaches. A practical man. No patience for philosophy, poetry, or conversations that take longer than the problem requires.

He understood two things with absolute clarity: that Irla of Rin was the finest weapon The Continent had ever produced, and that she had no interest whatsoever in being a weapon.

He spent twenty years attempting to reconcile these two facts. In the twenty-first year, he stopped trying. It was, by then, too late.

He was not a bad man. He simply mistook the weapon for the war.
