KELAK THE SWIFT

"It is said of the warrior Kelak, whom many called 'Kelak the Swift', that even the wind gave way when he ran. It was also said that he could make the same wind sing to the dance of his sword."

It was Kelak who warned first of The Hordes of Un. It was he, of The Seed Lands, near the great canyons of The Cut, that saw the horizon change.

He ran to get Irla. Found her absconding. Aided their escape.

Sound fella.

Warrior who was famous for being very, very fast.
