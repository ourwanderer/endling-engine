THE OOKAMI
/ oh-KAH-mee /
plural noun — warrior class / television property
A feared group of celebrity warriors. Sponsored, televised, and operating under the authority of Shogun Leiko and War Baron Niita. Their function is the public pursuit and capture of fugitives — or the elimination of threats to the established order.
Koa was the greatest of them. Her hunts drew the highest ratings in New Kyushu's recorded entertainment history. Her sponsors were generous. Her face was on things.

She left. She took something with her. The broadcasts have never quite recovered the same numbers.

They are still looking for her. The cameras are still running.
