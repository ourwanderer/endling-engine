DIMENSION ENGINE
/ dih-MEN-shun EN-jin /
noun - infrastructure / artefact of the Dead Gods
Mysterious giant contraptions that have something to do with dimensions.
The Engines stretch so far into the sky they blend into it. There's a special kind of silence around them , really it's not silent at all, and it's not just the wind. There's a sad song from it , a restless breath, like a keening ghost. The metal knocks and whines. But it's the uneasy whisper that haunts you.
