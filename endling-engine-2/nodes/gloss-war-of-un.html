THE WAR OF THE UN
/ THE WAR OF THE UN /
proper noun - historical event
The biggest tragedy to befall the Seed Lands.
Driven by famine from the Plains of Un, the nomadic people invaded the wealthy Seed Lands. They would not have prevailed had Irla involved herself in the fighting. But she absconded with her lover, Elayda, and the merchant class was destroyed.
