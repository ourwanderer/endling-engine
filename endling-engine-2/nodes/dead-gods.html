THE DEAD GODS
/ THUH DED GODZ /
plural noun — religion / historical fact
Central figures of religious belief on The Continent. They are referred to as dead not because they were worshipped and died, but because their absence behaves like death. Their creations persist. Their reasoning persists. Their purpose does not.
What remains: abandoned systems. Orphaned technologies. Artefacts without manuals. Symbols without interpreters.

The intelligences that built the Engines are no longer present. They are not named because names imply continuity, authorship, and intent — none of which can be reliably attributed.

They left. Everything else stayed.
