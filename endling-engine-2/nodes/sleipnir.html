SLEIPNIR
/ SLEYP-neer /
proper noun — vehicle / companion
Koa's motorcycle. Sentient, opinionated, and occasionally unreliable at the worst possible moments. The exact nature of its sentience is unclear. It does not speak. It communicates through behaviour — acceleration, hesitation, the precise timing of mechanical failures.
Named, presumably, after the eight-legged horse of Norse mythology. Whether Koa named it or it arrived with the name is not recorded.

It has never abandoned her. This is either loyalty or something stranger.
