SLEIPNIR
/ SLEYP-neer /
proper noun - vehicle / companion
Koa's faithful motorcycle. An early model of sentient bike, Sleipnir doesn't have the razzmatazz of later models like the Riastrad. But he'll get you where you need to go and he won't break down. Or at least , he won't stop even if he's a bit broken.
Named after the eight-legged horse of Norse mythology. Whether Koa named it or it arrived with the name is not recorded. It has never abandoned her.
