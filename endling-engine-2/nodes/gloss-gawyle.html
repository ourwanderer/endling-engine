GAWYLE, SCRIBE OF KREYTE
/ GAW-wyle /
proper noun - historian
Historian from the city of Kreyte in the Seed Lands.
Author of 'Warriors of The Auric Coast', 'A History of The Seed Lands: The Fall of The Corpocracy', and 'Champions of The Seed Lands'. A thorough chronicler of the events surrounding the War of Un and the warriors who shaped the Seed Lands.
