JIRO THE MONK
/ JEE-roh /
proper noun - historian / prophet
Strange little man, whose order believes 'gas' to be unnatural and so wear fake ones made of wood. As a result, most are dying of a condition called 'Blackbone', which is highly unpleasant and slowly, painfully, fatal. Something of a historian and, some say, prophet, Jiro wrote many texts on religious figures such as The Exile and The Oracle. Believes his own hype.
"In all the million lives I dream of, the one that frightens me most is the one where I fall towards the future like a mirror crashing to the floor." , From 'The Young Days of The Oracle' by Jiro the Monk.
