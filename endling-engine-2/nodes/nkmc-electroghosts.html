ELECTROGHOSTS

They rarely leave the neon-soaked streets of the capital. They don't need to. The city is their circuit.

Their bikes are wired differently — more processors, more sensors, more personality. The Electroghosts believe their machines are not just sentient but evolving. That given enough time, the bikes will be the riders.

They are probably right. They don't seem concerned by this.
