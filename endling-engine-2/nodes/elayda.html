GREEN TEMPLE — THE KEEPER

ELAYDA

Elayda of Green Temple. Keeper of the Holy Lines. A champion of the sacred city whose gifts were matched only by her scholarship.

She spent forty years cataloguing what the Dead Gods left behind — the residue of a civilisation that built the Engines and then, apparently, decided they were done. She understood the Engines before anyone had the language to describe them.

Her writings survive only in fragments. The Temple has been careful about that.

The Continent itself is infrastructure. She wrote this down. Nobody published it.
