THE GRANITE THRONE
/ GRAN-it THRONE /
proper noun - seat of power
Seat of the leaders in the city of Kreyte.
Bria's ascension to the Granite Throne was complete within a few short months, and she ruled until her death. Being my home, I have learned the story of Bria since my first days of schooling. Even now, centuries later, she still strikes the same chord , one of perfect balance between fear and undying admiration. , Gawyle, Scribe of Kreyte.
