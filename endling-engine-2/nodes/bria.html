THE CONTINENT - THE GRANITE THRONE

BRIA OF KREYTE

Famous warrior from The Nightcutter mountains who gained control of the city of Kreyte & the Granite Throne after the War against the Un.

"It was said she hailed from the heart of The Nightcutters , indeed from the very peak of Heavenslain itself , though none knew the truth of it.

Whatever her origins, Bria was as formidable as those cold mountains, and as deadly.

Not long after the War against the Un, Bria settled in the city of Kreyte and swiftly went about the business of making it her own. Her ascension to the Granite Throne was complete within a few short months, and she ruled until her death."
