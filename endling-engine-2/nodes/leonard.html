THE MONSTER HUNTER

LEONARD

Leonard is a badass. He smokes something that gives off green smoke. He has a katana named Agnar. He hunts monsters.

He is also completely alone (apart from the monsters).

Leonard wanders a bleak, derelict land. The only signs of anything other than him and the monsters having been there are The Engines , colossal metal ruins that stretch so far into the sky they blend into it. They are places he absolutely does not want to go.

But Leonard has no choice. Because on top of being alone, and not really knowing who or what or why he is , he is also not in full control of his movements.

You are.

"Who am I? I'm Leonard. You can take your Knights of the Auric Coast, your Ookami, your 3Moons UltraF***s and throw them all into The Mourning Sea for all I give a f***. I'd take 'em all. 'Cause my name's Leonard & I kill monsters."
