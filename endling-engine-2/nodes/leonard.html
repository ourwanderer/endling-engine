THE MONSTER HUNTER

LEONARD

A lone monster hunter in a bleak, derelict world. He carries a katana called Agnar.

His actions are not entirely his own. Somewhere, people are watching. Voting. Deciding what happens next.

He has walked to the edge of an Engine.
Over the door, something was written.
He didn't understand it then.

MUNDUS EVENTUS, NON RERUM
