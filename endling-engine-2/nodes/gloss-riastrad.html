RIASTRAD
/ REE-as-trad /
noun - the violent rage / also: a motorcycle model
The violent rage that consumes Cú Chulainn when provoked enough. Also a model of sentient motorcycle popular among the Electroghosts faction of the NKMC.
The Riastrad motorcycle. Many 3Moons bikes are described as 'fast' but to label this bike with that seems trite. It is not fast, it is photon-quick. Riders have certainly reported ageing ten years on a short ride. When only 8 seconds have passed for onlookers.
