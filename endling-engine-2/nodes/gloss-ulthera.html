ULTHERA
/ ul-THEER-ah /
proper noun - legendary place
Legendary place where, according to custom, the Dead Gods disappeared after they became.. dead. No descriptions of what it's actually like exist. Often used as a negative descriptor, eg 'It was as cold as Ulthera & I froze me balls off.' So people think it's cold anyway.
May they live forever in dreams.
