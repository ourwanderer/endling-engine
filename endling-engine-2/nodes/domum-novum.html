THE ORIGIN POINT

DOMUM NOVUM

This is where it all begins. It is still a mystery. Not much has been uncovered.

A man — we must assume Peregrinus — is chosen for a special task. In completing the task, he will lose all of his memory. Knowing this mission is humanity's only hope of survival, he accepts the sacrifice. He says goodbye to his family and undertakes the task.

When he awakes, he does not know who or where he is. But he is troubled by three moons in the sky, ever-watching.

Rough cuts. Poor quality. Age has not been kind.
But these songs tell a story.
