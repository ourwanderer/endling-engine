THE ORIGIN POINT

DOMUM NOVUM

This is where it all begins. This is the Origin Point. It is still a mystery. Not much has been uncovered.

We know of a man, named Peregrinus. We know of songs that tell part of his story. We know that he was the first to witness the Three Moons. The first to behold the frightening majesty of The Nightcutter mountains.

A man (we must assume Peregrinus) is chosen for a special task. But in completing the task, he will lose all of his memory. Knowing that this mission is humanity's only hope of survival, he accepts the sacrifice. He says goodbye to his family, and undertakes the task.

When he awakes, he does not know who or where he is. But he is troubled by three moons in the sky, ever-watching.

Rough cuts, poor quality , age has not been kind.
