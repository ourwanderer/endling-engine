THE CONTINENT — THE SACRED CITY

GREEN TEMPLE

The sacred city. Where the Holy Lines converge. Where the Dead Gods' presence is still faintly felt, like heat from a fire that went out too long ago.

Elayda was its Keeper. She spent forty years cataloguing the residue of a civilization that built the Engines and then, apparently, decided they were done.

The Temple has knowledge it does not share. The Temple has knowledge it cannot share, because the language it was written in has not been spoken for millennia.

They are working on it.
