DOMUM NOVUM — THE FIRST

PEREGRINUS

He was told three things.

He would not return. He would lose his memory. He would be humanity's scout for a new home.

He said goodbye to his family believing he was humanity's last hope. When he arrived, he remembered none of this. He wandered for weeks believing himself alone.

The new world had three moons.
He thought it was beautiful.

He did not discover a future for humanity.
He became its first ancestor.
