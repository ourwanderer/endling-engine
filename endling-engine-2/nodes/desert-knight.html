THE CONTINENT - NAMELESS ROGUE

THE DESERT KNIGHT

Of her, very little is known. The Desert Knight has no name.

It is said she was once a great warrior, under the command of Bria in the City of Kreyte, but no one can confirm this for there are no records of her being there. Only rumour.

All that is known is she was rogue , no longer under the watchful eye of the 3Moons Guild, and roaming the wastelands in search of... what?

A death befitting her, possibly. Something impossible to find, certainly.

Sponsored knights roam the Badlands... No kill, no pay.
