HIGH WAIL ROOK — THE KNIGHT

IRLA OF RIN

The greatest knight The Continent had ever produced. She was not interested in being the greatest.

Her devotion was not to the Dead Gods, not to any Shogun, not to any cause given to her by men. It was specific and inconvenient and completely ungovernable.

She disappeared from recorded history on a Tuesday. The records do not mention where she went. They rarely do, when the truth is inconvenient.
