THE CONTINENT — THE CLEAVE OF RIN

IRLA OF RIN

The most famous warrior in The Seed Lands and perhaps the entire continent. Unparalleled in battle-prowess.

The story of Irla, also known as 'The Cleave of Rin', and the love affair that destroyed a nation, is one of the most famous and well-loved tales on The Continent. It has been told in many forms, and inspired many timeworn songs.

"O Where have you been, Irla of Rin? The Seed Lands are now only dust / All of us left to fate / From the Aught to the Wraith / And daemons that feed on the just / O Where have you been, Irla of Rin? / Thou who put heart before sword / That love was a blade / To send us to the grave / And the Dead Gods take Lady and Lord." — Ancient song of The Auric Coast

Infamous too, after abandoning her masters when The Seed Lands were invaded by the Hordes of Un, leaving in secret to forge a life of peace with her lover, Elayda of Green Temple.
