THE GREAT VERDANT
/ THUH GRAYT VER-dant /
proper noun - region
Huge swathe of land covered in jungle and rain forest. Contains hairless amphibious monkeys. Among other things.
Doran SonLogan's journey takes him through the jungles of The Great Verdant, to the lethal peaks of The Nightcutter mountains, to fame (or infamy) among the warriors of The Seed Lands.
