NEW KYUSHU MOTORCYCLE CLUB

NKMC

The factions of New Kyushu, organised by machine and creed. The bikes are not just vehicles. They are allegiances. They are theology on two wheels.

The factions have names. The Dead Gods, The Electroghosts, The PR Crew. Each has a crest. Each has a doctrine. Each has a way of dying that is specific to their beliefs.

Koa does not belong to any faction. This is, in New Kyushu, its own kind of statement.
