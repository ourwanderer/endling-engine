THE CONTINENT — THE HOUND

CÚ CHULAINN

The great hero of Irish mythology. Formerly known as Setana, who became the Hound of Culainn. Who defended Ulster against Queen Maebh and the rest of Ireland.

You can't have him in a story set in a different universe entirely.

You can when time isn't linear and all realities exist.

Every mythology is someone's history. In an infinite universe, all of them are true.
