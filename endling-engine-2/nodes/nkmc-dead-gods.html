NKMC - FACTION - OF THE DAWN WASTE

DEAD GODS

Of the Dawn Waste. They ride the wastelands beyond the city wall where the Gas is thickest and the Blackbone claims everyone eventually.

They believe the Dead Gods speak through the toxic air. That Blackbone is not a disease but a transformation. That the ventilator is a denial of communion.

Their bikes are dark. Their crests are darker.

Their lungs are not what they used to be.
