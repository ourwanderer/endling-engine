THE MOURNING SEA
/ MORN-ing SEE /
proper noun - geographical feature
Large inland sea in the northwest of The Continent. Choppy. Miserable.
"High Wail is wet and grey and cold, and The Souse is the wet, grey, cold part of it." , Doran. The town of Enon Derris sits on the shores of the Mourning Sea. Doran SonLogan grew up there.
