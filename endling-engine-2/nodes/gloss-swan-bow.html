THE SWAN BOW
/ THE SWAN BOW /
proper noun - weapon
Famous weapon that once belonged to Elayda of Green Temple but fell into the hands of Baiden the Archer.
How he got it is often debated. Some say it was a lover's gift, which would piss Irla off no end. Other's accuse Baiden of being a thief. Arrows fired from The Swan Bow do not miss.
