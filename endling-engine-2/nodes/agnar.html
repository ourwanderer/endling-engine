AGNAR

proper noun — artefact
A katana of unknown origin. Famous. The blade appears in the possession of Koa, the Endling, in New Kyushu — and separately in the possession of Leonard, the monster hunter, in a different reality entirely.
The same blade. Different hands. Different realities. Different centuries, possibly.
