THE EXILE

One of two beings prophesied to bring about either the end or beginning of all creation. The other is The Oracle.

"I am Is and Un. I am one of a pair of souls, indivisibly coupled by quantum entanglement , the Ying and Yang of all existence, the Aeons of Pleroma, the secret to being or unbeing.

We are able to 'travel' the infinite multiverse, 'be' the infinite variables of ourselves in every type of possible reality and unreality and in this moment I happen to be stuck on a b*st%rd asteroid (thanks to her), close (but not too close) to a supermassive black hole of my own creation which I have named 'The Part of Koa's Soul Only I Can See' in her honour.

And right now , I'm stuck here. But she will come soon, and here in the jaws of endless gravity, where spacetime is wrought like a sail in a hurricane , here at our Rubicon , is where the last die will be cast. And then we'll see what's what."

Mysterious character who is trapped on an asteroid. Speaks in a very grandiose way.
