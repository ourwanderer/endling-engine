ORIGIN UNKNOWN

THE EXILE

He spoke in complete sentences. He quoted from texts that had not yet been written. He knew the shape of things that had not yet happened.

If you asked him who he was, he would tell you everything. The beginning and almost-the-end. The whole arc of it, delivered with extraordinary patience and a certain sadness.

No one thought to ask for a very long time.
When they did, they were not sure they believed it.
That was correct.
