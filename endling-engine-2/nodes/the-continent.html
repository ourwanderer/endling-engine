THE WIDER WORLD

THE CONTINENT

A vast landmass with a name that suggests whoever named it never saw the rest of it. Diverse geography, climate, culture, and religion.

The Dead Gods are the primary religion. The Holy Lines are the source of Magiq. Green Temple is the sacred city. High Wail is the cold, wet part that nobody visits unless they have to.

The Seed Lands are in the south. They are fertile and politically complicated, which is to say they are exactly like every fertile place has always been.
