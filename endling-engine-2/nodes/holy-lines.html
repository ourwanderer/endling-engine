HOLY LINES
/ HOH-lee LYNZ /
plural noun - also: Sacred Lines, Majik
The source of Magiq on The Continent. Holy Lines are said to contain the voices of the Dead Gods. When harnessed correctly can bestow great power. Often used to connect warriors to their weapons. Also often are placebos that have no real use , many bedeck themselves in Lines that are merely props.
Elayda performed the Absolute Sacrilege, and removed her Lines from the Sight of Gods. They were never seen again.
