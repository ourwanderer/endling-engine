K'WATHLEGONX
/ k'-WATH-leh-gonks /
noun - monster
If a tree and a monster had a baby and it wanted to eat you. See also: Tree Bastard.
Leonard smokes the K'wathlegonx root because it slows his heart so he can focus. It fills his lungs and head with calm. It stops his dreams from drowning him. He replants a bit of the one he kills so more little Tree Bastards can grow. Custom, apparently.
