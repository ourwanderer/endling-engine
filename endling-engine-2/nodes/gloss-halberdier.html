HALBERDIER
/ HAL-ber-deer /
noun — soldier / guardian
Soldiers & guardians of Green Temple. AKA The Armour Crushers.
The Seed Lands are also the centre point of the Continent's religion — the worship of The Dead Gods of Ulthera, who gave them access to majik via The Holy Lines and built The Engines. The beating heart of this religion is the palace city of Green Temple. Its sanctity is fiercely protected by many — including the elite Cataphracts, Halberdiers & heroes like Adira of Rayse.
