BLACKBONE
/ BLAK-bohn /
noun — also: GAS
The toxic atmosphere of New Kyushu. Gas refers to the ambient air itself; Blackbone is the disease caused by prolonged exposure without adequate filtration. Symptoms progress from respiratory difficulty to skeletal calcification.
Everyone in New Kyushu wears a ventilator. The quality of your ventilator is the most reliable indicator of your social standing. The most expensive models are barely visible. The cheapest are bulky, inefficient, and leave the wearer with an unambiguous class marking across their face.

The Ookami wear custom models. Designer. Sponsored.
