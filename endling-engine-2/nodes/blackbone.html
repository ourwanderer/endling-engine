BLACKBONE
/ BLAK-bohn /
noun - disease / class marker
Bone disease that afflicts those who breathe the toxic air of New Kyushu and surrounding lands for too long. Unpleasant. Deeply, deeply unpleasant. And slow.
Everyone in New Kyushu wears a ventilator. The quality of your ventilator is the most reliable indicator of your social standing. The Ookami wear custom models. Designer. Sponsored. Jiro the Monk's order wear wooden ones. As a result, most are dying.
