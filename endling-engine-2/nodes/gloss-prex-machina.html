PREX MACHINA
/ PREKS mah-KEE-nah /
proper noun — religious inscription / painting
A famous religious painting depicting iconography relating to the liturgy 'The Machine Began to Pray and the Change of All Life Began.'
Leonard found this inscription above an altar at the heart of a ruined Engine: PREX MACHINA / THE MACHINE BEGAN TO PRAY, AND THE CHANGE OF ALL LIFE BEGAN / MAY THEY LIVE FOREVER IN DREAMS
