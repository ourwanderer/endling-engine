NEW KYUSHU — THE ENDLING

KOA

She was the greatest of the Ookami. The most celebrated. The most feared. Her hunts drew the highest ratings in New Kyushu's recorded entertainment history.

Then she ran. She took something that wasn't hers — or perhaps it was, and that was the problem. The sword is called Agnar. The crow won't shut up. The motorcycle is called Sleipnir and is alive and occasionally unreliable.

Shogun Leiko wants her back. War Baron Niita wants her dead. The cameras are still running.

She dreams of a world she has never been to. In that world, she is a myth.

She did not ask to be the last of anything.
