3MOONS
/ THREE MOONZ /
proper noun — corporation / phenomenon
An ultra-corporation producing everything. A mysterious guild controlling the Knights. Also the three actual moons visible in the sky of Domum Novum — ever-watching, ever-present, unexplained.
The corporation named itself after the phenomenon, or the phenomenon was named after the corporation, or the name crossed realities with Peregrinus and embedded itself in a commercial entity ten thousand years later without anyone noticing.

This kind of thing happens more than it should.
