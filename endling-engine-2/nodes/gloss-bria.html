BRIA OF KREYTE
/ BREE-ah of KREET /
proper noun - warrior / ruler
Famous warrior from The Nightcutter mountains who gained control of the city of Kreyte & the Granite Throne after the War against the Un.
"It was said she hailed from the heart of The Nightcutters , indeed from the very peak of Heavenslain itself , though none knew the truth of it. Whatever her origins, Bria was as formidable as those cold mountains, and as deadly. Even now, centuries later, she still strikes the same chord , one of perfect balance between fear and undying admiration." , From 'A History of The Seed Lands' by Gawyle, Scribe of Kreyte.
