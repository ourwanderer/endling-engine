THE NIGHTCUTTERS
/ NYTE-kut-erz /
plural noun - mountain range
Vast mountain range that stretch from The Night Waste to The Embers. Contains the highest peak on The Continent , Heavenslain.
"Three moons, ever watching / Three peaks underneath / Each peak afire / Can I get no relief?" , The Words of Peregrinus. Are these those famous peaks? Seen for the first time? Before they had a name?
