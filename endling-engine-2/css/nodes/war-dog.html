NEW KYUSHU — THE WOLF

WAR DOG

Koa's wolf. White, large, and extraordinarily calm about things that should not be calm about.

He has been with her since before the Ookami. Before Agnar. Before Doran. His origins are unknown and he has never volunteered the information.

He does not dream of other worlds. He does not need to. He is already exactly where he is supposed to be.

He will not leave her. This is not in question.
