THE CITY-NATION

NEW KYUSHU

A massive, technologically advanced city-nation. Feudalist in structure, consumerist in culture, warrior-class in mythology.

The air is toxic. Everyone wears a ventilator. Those who cannot afford a good one get Blackbone eventually. Most people cannot afford a good one.

The Ookami are televised. Their hunts are sponsored. The celebrities of New Kyushu are the people who kill other people, professionally and on camera.

Koa was the most celebrated of them.
Then she took something that wasn't hers.
Then she ran.
