DOMUM NOVUM — THE FIRST

PEREGRINUS

He was told three things. He would not return. He would lose his memory. He would be humanity's scout for a new home.

He said goodbye to his family believing he was humanity's last hope. When he arrived, he remembered none of this. He wandered. He found a world with three moons. He thought it was beautiful.

He did not discover a future for humanity. He became its first ancestor. The world he found was called Domum Novum. The three moons watch it still.

May he live forever in dreams.
