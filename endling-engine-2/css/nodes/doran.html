NEW KYUSHU — THE CROW / HIGH WAIL ROOK — THE BOY

DORAN

Koa's crow. Foul-mouthed, loyal, and considerably smarter than he lets on. He has opinions about most things and will share them whether asked or not.

In another reality — The Continent — there is a young man also called Doran. He is from a fishing village in the Souse, the cold and wet part of High Wail Rook. He dreams of Koa. He does not know what she is.

Both Dorans are moving toward the same point from different directions. Neither of them knows this yet.

Myths survive Engine traversal better than facts. Doran has survived everything.
