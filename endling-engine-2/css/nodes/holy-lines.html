HOLY LINES
/ HOH-lee LYNZ /
plural noun — also: MAGIQ
The source of Magiq on The Continent. Believed to contain the voices of the Dead Gods. Magiq is the manipulation of these lines to achieve extraordinary feats — healing, destruction, traversal, communication across impossible distances.
Elayda's theory, never published in her lifetime: the Holy Lines are not voices. They are code. The Dead Gods did not speak into them — they built them. The Continent itself is infrastructure.

The implications of this were, in her words, "too large to be useful and too accurate to be ignored."
