THE CONTINENT — THE CLEAVE OF RIN

IRLA

A legendary warrior from The Seed Lands. Renowned throughout The Continent for her exceptional skill in battle — a reputation she finds tedious.

Her story is inseparable from Elayda of Green Temple. The famous and tragic love affair between the champion of the temple city and the finest blade The Continent ever produced is the kind of story that gets written down, then copied, then argued over.

Irla does not argue about it. She does not discuss it. She is still, in certain circles, looking.

The records do not say where she went. They rarely do, when the truth is inconvenient.
