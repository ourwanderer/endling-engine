WHAT IS THIS?

THE ENDLING SAGA

This is an ongoing transmedia project — a story told across images, fragments, music, and whatever form seems right at the time. It is set in an infinite universe. All realities exist. Some are stable. Some bleed into each other.

The worlds reveal themselves in pieces. They show more to those who look further. There is no correct order, and no finish line. If one fragment stays with you, that is enough. If you follow threads for weeks, that is welcome too.

The site is the work. The Engine is not a metaphor.

Made by Matt Griffin — illustrator and author, based in Ireland. Commercial work and professional context at mattgriffin.art.
